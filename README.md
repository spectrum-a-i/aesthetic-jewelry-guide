# Aesthetic Jewelry Style Guide

Jewelry can completely change the mood of an outfit.

A simple necklace can make a casual look feel more polished. A pearl earring can add a romantic detail, while a sparkling moissanite ring can create a more dramatic effect.

This guide explores different jewelry aesthetics and how to style them.

## Pearl Aesthetic

Pearls don't have to look traditional.

Baroque pearls, drop earrings and pearl rings can create a softer, more contemporary aesthetic. They work particularly well with neutral colors, flowing fabrics and minimalist outfits.

## Celestial Aesthetic

Stars, wings, butterflies and moon-inspired designs create a dreamy aesthetic that works especially well with evening outfits.

Moissanite can add another dimension because of its strong sparkle and ability to catch the light as you move.

## Minimalist Aesthetic

Minimalist jewelry is about choosing fewer pieces and letting each one have a purpose.

A delicate necklace, small earrings or one distinctive ring can be enough to complete an outfit.

## Colorful Jewelry

Jewelry doesn't always need to be neutral.

Amethyst, colorful gemstones and rainbow-inspired designs can add personality to simple outfits and create contrast with monochrome clothing.

## Building a Personal Jewelry Collection

You don't need to follow one aesthetic.

Mix pearls with modern pieces. Combine delicate jewelry with one statement ring. Wear celestial designs with everyday clothing.

The goal is not to make every piece match.

The goal is to create a collection that feels personal.

## Final Thought

The best jewelry aesthetic isn't necessarily the one that's trending.

It's the one that feels natural when you wear it.

Your jewelry should complement your style, express your personality and, most importantly, make you want to wear it again tomorrow.
